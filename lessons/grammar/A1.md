# A1 English Grammar: 20 Classes (Smart Question Set)

**All 36 topics covered. One class = one lesson (45-60 minutes).**

**Question formation in every class (10 questions):**
- ★ **Easy:** 1 question (10%)
- ★★ **Medium:** 2 questions (20%)
- ★★★ **Hard / tweaked:** 7 questions (70%). These look easy but contain a trap. A learner who only half-knows the rule will get them wrong. The answer key explains each trap.

## Class 1: To be + word order

**Today you will learn**
- am / is / are in positive, negative and question forms
- Short answers (Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.)
- Basic order: Subject + Verb + Object (+ place + time)

**Questions**
1. ★ I ___ happy today.
2. ★★ Make negative: "He is a teacher."
3. ★★ Put in order: is / where / the station / ?
4. ★★★ "Are you a student?" Which short answer is correct? (a) Yes, I'm. (b) Yes, I am. (c) Yes, I are.
5. ★★★ My brother and I ___ late. (is / am / are)
6. ★★★ Which one is WRONG? (a) We aren't tired. (b) We're not tired. (c) We not are tired.
7. ★★★ Put in order: to school / goes / every day / Tom
8. ★★★ My glasses ___ on the table. (is / are)
9. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "I have 20 years."
10. ★★★ Which order is correct? (a) Maria likes very much music. (b) Maria likes music very much. (c) Maria very much likes music.

**Answer key**
1. am
2. He isn't a teacher. / He's not a teacher.
3. Where is the station?
4. (b). Positive short answers are never contracted (not "Yes, I'm").
5. are. "My brother and I" = we.
6. (c). Negative "not" comes after "be".
7. Tom goes to school every day. (Place before time.)
8. are. "Glasses" is plural even though it is one object.
9. I am 20 (years old). English uses "be", not "have", for age.
10. (b). The object comes straight after the verb.

**Can you do it?** I can say who or what someone is, and build a simple sentence in the right order.

## Class 2: This, that, these, those + this vs it

**Today you will learn**
- This/these = near; that/those = far
- Singular vs plural
- "This" to point and introduce; "it" to talk about something already named

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ (near) are my keys. (This / These)
2. ★★ "What's ___ over there?" (this / that)
3. ★★ Make plural: "This is a bus."
4. ★★★ You hold a pen: "___ is my pen. ___ is a good pen." (This / It)
5. ★★★ You point at cars far away: "___ cars are expensive." (That / Those)
6. ★★★ On the phone you ask who is calling: "Hello, who is ___?" (this / that)
7. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "Those is my bag."
8. ★★★ You hold two shoes: "How much are ___?" (this / these)
9. ★★★ "Look at that dog. ___ is so big!" (This / It)
10. ★★★ "___ is raining and ___ is cold today." (This/It)

**Answer key**
1. These
2. that
3. These are buses.
4. This, It. The first mention points ("this"); after that we say "it".
5. Those
6. that. You ask "Who is that?"; you introduce yourself with "This is Anna."
7. That is my bag. / Those are my bags.
8. these. "Shoes" is plural.
9. It. The dog is already named.
10. It, it. Weather always uses "it".

**Can you do it?** I can point at things near and far and refer back to them with "it".

## Class 3: Pronouns and possessive adjectives

**Today you will learn**
- Subject pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- Possessive adjectives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their
- Object pronouns: me, you, him, her, it, us, them

**Questions**
1. ★ My parents are here. ___ are nice. (They / Them)
2. ★★ Maria and Tom are friends. ___ live in Paris. (They / Them)
3. ★★ This is my sister. ___ name is Eva. (She / Her)
4. ★★★ The dog hurt ___ leg. (its / it's)
5. ★★★ "Give ___ the keys, please." (said to you by Anna and me) (we / us)
6. ★★★ "Anna and ___ went home." (I / me)
7. ★★★ I like Sam, but he doesn't like ___. (I / me)
8. ★★★ I know Dan, but I don't know ___ sister. (he / him / his)
9. ★★★ You and I are friends. ___ are happy. (We / Us)
10. ★★★ Tom and Eva have a dog. ___ dog is called Max. (There / They're / Their)

**Answer key**
1. They
2. They
3. Her
4. its. "It's" always means "it is".
5. us. After a verb or "to" you need an object pronoun.
6. I. "Anna and I" is the subject. Test: "I went home."
7. me. It comes after the verb "like".
8. his. A noun (sister) needs a possessive adjective, not a pronoun.
9. We
10. Their. It shows ownership. "There" = place, "They're" = they are.

**Can you do it?** I can choose the right pronoun or possessive for who does or receives the action.

## Class 4: Whose, possessive 's + have got

**Today you will learn**
- "Whose is this?" / "It's Mike's."
- 's for one owner, s' for plural owners
- have got / has got

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ phone is this? (Who's / Whose)
2. ★★ "These are the girls' coats." How many girls are there?
3. ★★ He ___ got two sisters. (have / has)
4. ★★★ "Peter's got a dog." What does 's mean here? (is / has / belongs to)
5. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "The childrens' toys are old."
6. ★★★ The room belongs to two boys. "It's the ___ room." (boy's / boys')
7. ★★★ "Have you got a pen?" "No, ___." (a) I haven't got. (b) I haven't. (c) I don't.
8. ★★★ "___ at the door?" "It's the postman." (Whose / Who's)
9. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "Has you got a pen?"
10. ★★★ Rewrite with 's: "the book of my sister"

**Answer key**
1. Whose
2. More than one. The apostrophe comes after the s (girls').
3. has
4. has. Peter has got a dog. 's can mean is, has, or possession.
5. The children's toys are old. "Children" is already plural, so add 's.
6. boys'. Two boys = s'.
7. (b). The short answer ends at "haven't".
8. Who's. It means "who is".
9. Have you got a pen?
10. my sister's book

**Can you do it?** I can say who things belong to and what I have.

## Class 5: A/an, plurals + the / no article

**Today you will learn**
- a before consonant SOUNDS, an before vowel SOUNDS
- Regular and irregular plurals
- When to use a/an, the, or no article

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ egg (a / an)
2. ★★ Plural of "city"
3. ★★ Plural of "child"
4. ★★★ ___ hour (a / an)
5. ★★★ ___ university (a / an)
6. ★★★ Plural of knife, tooth, sheep
7. ★★★ "I like ___ pizza. ___ pizza on the table is cold." (the / no article)
8. ★★★ He is ___ honest man. (a / an)
9. ★★★ She plays ___ guitar and ___ football. (the / no article)
10. ★★★ "I had ___ breakfast at 8." (a / the / no article)

**Answer key**
1. an
2. cities
3. children
4. an. The h is silent, so it starts with a vowel sound.
5. a. It starts with a "y" sound (/j/).
6. knives, teeth, sheep (sheep does not change)
7. no article, The. In general = no article; a specific pizza = the.
8. an. Silent h again.
9. the guitar, no article football. Instruments take "the"; sports do not.
10. no article. Meals take no article.

**Can you do it?** I can use a/an/the correctly and make plurals.

## Class 6: Adjectives + adverbs of manner

**Today you will learn**
- Adjectives go before nouns and after "be"; they never change for plural
- Adverbs end in -ly (quick → quickly)
- Irregular: good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard

**Questions**
1. ★ She is a ___ singer. (good / well)
2. ★★ He runs very ___. (fast / fastly)
3. ★★ Make an adverb from "angry".
4. ★★★ She looks ___. (happy / happily)
5. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "I have two reds cars."
6. ★★★ He works very ___. (hard / hardly)
7. ★★★ She speaks English very ___. (good / well)
8. ★★★ "Drive ___!" (careful / carefully)
9. ★★★ I got up ___ this morning, so I missed the bus. (late / lately)
10. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) a red small car (b) a small red car

**Answer key**
1. good. It describes a noun (singer).
2. fast. "Fastly" does not exist.
3. angrily
4. happy. After "look/feel/smell/be" we use an adjective.
5. two red cars. Adjectives do not take -s.
6. hard. "Hardly" means almost not.
7. well. It describes the verb "speaks".
8. carefully
9. late. "Lately" means recently.
10. (b). Size comes before colour.

**Can you do it?** I can describe things and say how people do things.

## Class 7: Present simple

**Today you will learn**
- Present simple for habits, routines, facts
- he/she/it + -s / -es / -ies
- don't/doesn't + base verb; do/does questions

**Questions**
1. ★ She ___ (like) tea.
2. ★★ My sister ___ (study) English.
3. ★★ Make negative: "She works on Sundays."
4. ★★★ My father ___ (watch) the news every night.
5. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) He don't know. (b) He doesn't knows. (c) He doesn't know.
6. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) Does she goes? (b) Do she go? (c) Does she go?
7. ★★★ My sister ___ (try) hard and my brother ___ (play) a lot.
8. ★★★ What time ___ the film start? (do / does)
9. ★★★ My cat ___ (not/like) milk.
10. ★★★ She ___ (have) a shower at 7.

**Answer key**
1. likes
2. studies
3. She doesn't work on Sundays.
4. watches. Words ending in -ch add -es.
5. (c). After doesn't, the verb has no -s.
6. (c). "Does" already carries the -s.
7. tries, plays. Consonant + y → -ies; vowel + y → -s.
8. does. "Film" = it.
9. doesn't like
10. has. Irregular: have → has.

**Can you do it?** I can talk about my daily life and facts.

## Class 8: Questions + adverbs of frequency

**Today you will learn**
- Order: Question word + do/does + subject + verb
- who, what, where, when, why, how, what time
- always, usually, often, sometimes, never, and where they go

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ is your name? (What / Who)
2. ★★ "___ do you live?" "In Rome." (Where / When)
3. ★★ Add "never": "I am late."
4. ★★★ Put in order: does / what time / she / get up
5. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) Where she lives? (b) Where does she live? (c) Where does she lives?
6. ★★★ "___ is that man?" "He's my uncle." (What / Who)
7. ★★★ Add "sometimes": "I am tired on Mondays."
8. ★★★ "How ___ do you go to the cinema?" "Twice a month." (much / many / often)
9. ★★★ What does "I don't always eat breakfast" mean? (a) I never eat breakfast. (b) I sometimes eat breakfast.
10. ★★★ "Who ___ (like) pizza?" (Use present simple.)

**Answer key**
1. What
2. Where
3. I am never late. (After "be".)
4. What time does she get up?
5. (b)
6. Who. We ask about people with "who".
7. I am sometimes tired on Mondays.
8. often. "How often" asks about frequency.
9. (b). "Not always" does not mean "never".
10. likes. When "who" is the subject, no do/does is used, and the verb takes -s.

**Can you do it?** I can ask questions and say how often I do things.

## Class 9: Present continuous + present simple or continuous?

**Today you will learn**
- am/is/are + verb-ing for actions now
- Negatives and questions
- Present simple (routine) vs present continuous (now / temporary)

**Questions**
1. ★ I ___ (study) now.
2. ★★ What ___ she doing? (is / does)
3. ★★ Add -ing to "write".
4. ★★★ I ___ (know) the answer.
5. ★★★ Be quiet! The baby ___ (sleep).
6. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) Do you working? (b) Are you working? (c) You are working?
7. ★★★ Tom usually ___ (wear) a suit, but today he ___ (wear) jeans.
8. ★★★ Why ___ (you / laugh)?
9. ★★★ I ___ (not / understand) this word.
10. ★★★ Look! She ___ (have) lunch now.

**Answer key**
1. am studying
2. is
3. writing (drop the e)
4. know. "Know" is a state verb and is not used in the continuous.
5. is sleeping
6. (b). It is a question, so "Are" comes first.
7. wears, is wearing
8. are you laughing
9. don't understand. State verbs are not used in the continuous.
10. is having. Here "have" means eat, so the continuous is OK ("She has a car" would not be).

**Can you do it?** I can say what is happening now and what happens regularly.

## Class 10: The imperative + can/can't

**Today you will learn**
- Imperatives: Sit down! Don't talk!
- can/can't for ability, possibility, permission
- Questions with can

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ (close) the window, please.
2. ★★ Make negative: "Open the door."
3. ★★ She ___ play the piano. (can / cans)
4. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "She can to swim."
5. ★★★ Make negative: "Be late."
6. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) Do you can swim? (b) Can you swim?
7. ★★★ "Can I sit here?" What does it ask for? (a) ability (b) permission
8. ★★★ Please ___ (not / touch) the paintings.
9. ★★★ I ___ hear you. (negative of can)
10. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "He cans speak French."

**Answer key**
1. Close
2. Don't open the door.
3. can
4. She can swim. "Can" is followed by the base verb, never "to".
5. Don't be late. The negative imperative of "be" uses "don't" too.
6. (b)
7. (b). It is a request for permission, not about ability.
8. Don't touch
9. can't
10. He can speak French. "Can" never takes -s.

**Can you do it?** I can give instructions, say what I can/can't do, and ask permission.

## Class 11: At, in, on (time and place) + position words

**Today you will learn**
- Time: at (clock, night), in (months, years, parts of day), on (days, dates)
- Place: at (point), in (inside), on (surface)
- next to, under, between, in front of, behind, over

**Questions**
1. ★ The class starts ___ 5 o'clock. (at / in / on)
2. ★★ I was born ___ 1999. (at / in / on)
3. ★★ See you ___ Friday. (at / in / on)
4. ★★★ I work ___ night and sleep ___ the afternoon. (at / in / on)
5. ★★★ My birthday is ___ 5th May. (at / in / on)
6. ★★★ We're ___ the bus and she's ___ bed. (at / in / on)
7. ★★★ A bridge goes ___ the river. (over / on)
8. ★★★ I'm sitting ___ you, on your right. (between / next to)
9. ★★★ Cars wait ___ the traffic lights. (at / in / on)
10. ★★★ We have a party ___ Christmas Day, but we give presents ___ Christmas. (at / on)

**Answer key**
1. at
2. in
3. on
4. at, in. The exception: "at night" but "in the afternoon".
5. on. Specific dates use "on".
6. on, in. You travel on a bus; you are in bed.
7. over
8. next to
9. at. Traffic lights are a point.
10. on, at. A specific day = on; the festival period = at.

**Can you do it?** I can say when and where things happen and where they are.

## Class 12: There is/are + there or it

**Today you will learn**
- There is / are; there was / were
- Questions and negatives
- There (something exists) vs It (weather, time, a thing already named)

**Questions**
1. ★ ___ a book on the table. (There is / There are)
2. ★★ ___ three chairs in the room. (There is / There are)
3. ★★ Make a question: "There is a bank near here."
4. ★★★ "___ a pen and two books on the desk." (There is / There are)
5. ★★★ "___ is hot today." (It / There)
6. ★★★ "___ are 30 students in my class." (They / It / There)
7. ★★★ I saw "Titanic". ___ was very long. (It / There)
8. ★★★ ___ was a good film on TV last night. (It / There)
9. ★★★ How many people ___ there at the party? (was / were)
10. ★★★ There ___ any eggs. (negative) (isn't / aren't)

**Answer key**
1. There is
2. There are
3. Is there a bank near here?
4. There is. We match the first noun (a pen) in a list.
5. It. Weather uses "it".
6. There. We say "there are" to say something exists.
7. It. The film was already named.
8. There. A new thing is introduced.
9. were
10. aren't. "Eggs" is plural.

**Can you do it?** I can describe what exists in a place and talk about weather and time.

## Class 13: A/some/any + quantity words

**Today you will learn**
- Countable (apples) and uncountable (milk, advice)
- some / any
- much, many, a lot of, a little, a few

**Questions**
1. ★ I'd like ___ water. (some / any)

3. ★★ How ___ money do you have? (much / many)

**Can you do it?** I can talk about amounts and ask how much or how many.

## Class 15: Past simple, regular and irregular verbs

**Today you will learn**
- Regular: -ed, -ied, doubled letter
- Common irregular verbs
- Past time words: yesterday, last week, two days ago

**Questions**
1. ★ Yesterday I ___ (play) tennis.
2. ★★ Past of "cry"
3. ★★ Past of "stop"
4. ★★★ Past of "visit"
5. ★★★ Past of "read" (write it)
6. ★★★ Past of "teach" and "catch"
7. ★★★ Which -ed ending has a /t/ sound? (a) played (b) washed (c) wanted

9. ago
10. drank, ate

## Class 18: Comparative and superlative adjectives

**Today you will learn**
- Short: -er / -est
- Long: more / the most
- Irregular: good, better, best; bad, worse, worst
- than

**Questions**
1. ★ Tom is ___ than Anna. (tall)
2. ★★ Comparative of "big"
3. ★★ Comparative of "happy"

## Class 19: Will/shall + be going to

**Today you will learn**
- will for quick decisions, promises, predictions
- shall for offers/suggestions
- be going to for plans and predictions based on evidence

**Questions**
1. ★ I ___ call you tomorrow. (will / shall)
2. ★★ It's cold. I ___ close the window. (will / am going to)
3. ★★ I ___ see my uncle tomorrow. (I made the plan last week.) (will / am going to)
4. ★★★ Look at the sky! It ___ rain. (will / is going to)
5. ★★★ I ___ tell anyone. I promise. (won't / am not going to)
6. ★★★ ___ I open the window? (Shall / Will)
7. ★★★ Correct the sentence: "I will to go."
8. ★★★ The phone rings. "I ___ answer it!" (will / am going to)
9. ★★★ She ___ be a doctor. (She has decided.) (will / is going to)

1. will
2. will. It is a quick decision made now.
3. am going to. It is a plan made before.
4. is going to. There is evidence now (the sky).
5. won't. It is a promise.
6. Shall. It is an offer with I/we.
7. I will go.
8. will. It is a decision made at that moment.
9. is going to
10. (b)

## Class 20: Conjunctions + final review

**Today you will learn**
- and (adds), but (contrast), or (choice), so (result), because (reason)
- To join short sentences
- To review everything in short mixed sentences

**Questions**
1. ★ I like tea ___ coffee. (and / but)
2. ★★ It was cold, ___ I wore a coat. (so / because)
3. ★★ I was late ___ the bus broke down. (so / because)
4. ★★★ I'm tired ___ I'm happy. (and / but)
5. ★★★ I can't come ___ I'm busy. (so / because)
6. ★★★ I'm busy, ___ I can't come. (so / because)
7. ★★★ I don't like tea ___ coffee. (and / or)
8. ★★★ She is poor ___ she is happy. (so / but)
9. ★★★ Which is correct? (a) He go to school yesterday and see his friends. (b) He went to school yesterday and saw his friends.
10. ★★★ Yesterday I was tired, ___ I went to bed early. (so / because)

**Answer key**
1. and
2. so. Cold is the reason; the coat is the result.
3. because. The bus breaking down is the reason.
4. but. Two opposite ideas.
5. because. "Because" introduces the reason (busy).
6. so. "So" introduces the result (can't come).
7. or. After a negative, we use "or".
8. but
9. (b). Both verbs must be in the past.
10. so. Tired is the reason; going to bed is the result.

**Can you do it?** I can link ideas and write a short, correct paragraph.
