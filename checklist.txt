4. MEME GENERATOR QUALITY & RATE LIMIT GUARANTEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] MEME GENERATION CHECKLIST
    - Origin: "filter this type of generated meme (no image in background/not
      generated perfectly) create another script inside meme_generator called
      meme_validator and validate the generated meme before showing the user...
      generate another instead of showing this... generete 1 meme per click...
      currently we are creating 3 memes..."
    - Quality & Rules:
      * 1 meme per click enforced (avoid creating 3 memes).
      * Rate limiting: 15 memes per day quota.
      * Session cooldown: 3 memes in a session trigger a 30-minute cooldown.
      * Defective Placeholder Detection (`backend/meme_generator/meme_validator.py`):
        Filters out Memegen striped placeholders (mean_sat < 8.0, channel_diff < 4.0).
        Automatically retries with verified backup templates until a valid image exists.
      * Pixel Cropping (`backend/meme_generator/meme_crop.py`):
        Removes bottom 14px footer watermark before presenting image to user.
